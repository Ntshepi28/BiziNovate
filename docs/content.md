# About BiziNovate

## Company Overview

BiziNovate ICT Academy is an organisation committed to empowering individuals and communities through accessible technology education, digital skills development, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

The academy aims to bridge the digital divide by providing practical training, mentorship, and career-focused learning opportunities that prepare learners for the digital economy.

---

# Vision

# Learning Model

BiziNovate delivers practical bootcamps designed to combine theory with hands-on projects.

Each programme includes:

- Instructor-led sessions
- Practical exercises
- Team projects
- Assessments
- Mentorship
- Certificate of Completion

---

# Certificate

Learners who successfully complete a bootcamp and meet the assessment requirements receive a Certificate of Completion from BiziNovate ICT Academy.
