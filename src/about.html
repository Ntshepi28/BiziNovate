About BiziNovate

Building a Future Where Everyone Has the Opportunity to Thrive Through Technology

BiziNovate is a non-profit organisation dedicated to empowering disadvantaged youth and women through digital literacy, IT training, entrepreneurship programmes, mentorship, and innovation. We believe technology can create opportunities, transform communities, and build a more inclusive digital future.

Our Story

Why BiziNovate Was Founded

BiziNovate was founded to address the growing digital divide affecting many South African communities. Too many talented young people and women lack access to quality technology education and opportunities, preventing them from reaching their full potential.

By providing practical digital literacy, IT training, entrepreneurship programmes, mentorship, and community initiatives, BiziNovate creates pathways into education, employment, and innovation.

Today, BiziNovate continues to expand its impact by delivering innovative technology programmes that inspire creativity, promote entrepreneurship, and prepare future technology professionals.

Our Mission

To empower youth, women, and underserved communities through accessible, practical technology education that creates employment opportunities, encourages innovation, and supports entrepreneurship.

Our Vision

To become one of Africa's leading technology organisations, building digitally empowered communities capable of driving innovation, economic growth, and sustainable development.
