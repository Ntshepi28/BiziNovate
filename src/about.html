About BiziNovate ICT Academy

Empowering communities through technology, innovation, entrepreneurship, and digital inclusion.

Our Story

BiziNovate ICT Academy was established to bridge the digital divide by providing accessible technology education and practical digital skills. Our goal is to equip young people and underserved communities with the knowledge needed to thrive in the digital economy.

Our Vision

To become a leading ICT academy that transforms lives through innovation, technology, and inclusive education.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality technology training, mentorship, and career development opportunities that prepare learners for the future workforce.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Inclusion

  • Excellence
  • Community Empowerment
